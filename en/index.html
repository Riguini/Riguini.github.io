About me

I am a data analyst and Power BI developer based in Toronto. I have worked with Power BI since 2021 and have training in data science.

My work starts before the dashboard: understanding the business, organizing the data and defining the questions that need answers. Then come the data model, the DAX measures and a layout designed for the people who use the report every day.

Each portfolio project simulates a company and a real problem, from an IFRS 18 income statement to a marketing acquisition funnel, always with the same goal: drive decisions, not just charts.
